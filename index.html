⚔ DUNGEON OF MINDS: CHARACTERS, RULES & AI ARCHITECTURE

⚔ Warrior (Minimax + Alpha-Beta Pruning)

Stats: 100 HP, 20 Base ATK.
Abilities:
• Slash: Adjacent melee strike dealing 20 damage with 90% accuracy.
• Block: Defensive stance granting 50% damage reduction until next turn.
AI Decision Engine: Minimax adversarial lookahead with Alpha-Beta pruning (α-β), assuming optimal opponent counters and maximizing worst-case outcomes.

🔮 Mage (Expectimax Search)

Stats: 70 HP, 10 Base ATK.
Abilities:
• Magic Missile: Ranged projectile (range 3) dealing 10 damage with 100% hit rate.
• Fireball: Devastating blast (range 4) dealing 25 damage with 80% accuracy and a 2-turn cooldown.
AI Decision Engine: Expectimax search evaluating probabilistic chance nodes (accuracy percentages and critical strikes) to maximize expected utility.

👺 Goblins (Finite State Machine - FSM)

Stats: 20 HP, 5 ATK. Roam the 10x10 dungeon as environmental hazards and target whichever hero is closer.
FSM States:
• Patrol: Circles procedural room waypoints.
• Chase: Engages A* pursuit when a hero approaches within 4 tiles.
• Attack: Strikes adjacent hero for 5 damage.
• Flee: Retreats when HP drops to ≤ 5.

🗺 Dungeon Environment & Turn Rules

Turn Order: Warrior → Mage → Goblins → Next Round.
Actions: Move up to 2 tiles, attack or cast, block (Warrior only), or wait.
Tiles: Wall (blocks movement & spell line-of-sight), Trap (10 HP damage), Potion (+20 HP restore once).
Win Condition: Reduce opponent to 0 HP. If neither wins within 40 rounds, the hero with higher remaining HP wins!

🧭 PATHFINDING ALGORITHMS

A* Search (Astar)

Uses evaluation function f(n) = g(n) + h(n) where g(n) is actual distance traveled and h(n) is the Manhattan distance heuristic. Integrated with dynamic hazard costs to intelligently detour around discovered traps while guaranteeing shortest path optimality.

Dijkstra's Algorithm (Uniform Cost)

Explores outwards symmetrically by cost without any heuristic guide. Guarantees the optimal shortest path, but explores significantly more nodes than A*, resulting in higher search footprint.

Greedy Best-First Search

Rushes directly towards the target based strictly on h(n). While extremely fast in open spaces, it is susceptible to local minima and can take heavily suboptimal detours around walls and hazards.

Breadth-First Search (BFS)

Exhaustively searches in level-order FIFO queue. Finds shortest path in grid step count, but expands all radial branches regardless of goal direction.

⚔ COMBAT DECISION ALGORITHMS

Minimax with Alpha-Beta Pruning

Game-theoretic adversarial search that assumes the opponent will always pick their optimal countermove. Prunes branches where β ≤ α, allowing deep 4-turn lookahead within milliseconds.

Expectimax Search

Models non-deterministic game elements (critical strikes with 12% probability and damage variance) as chance nodes, maximizing expected utility rather than assuming worst-case scenarios.

Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)

Performs rapid random playout rollouts to terminal states. Aggregates win frequencies for candidate actions without needing hand-crafted heuristic weights.

Rule-Based Expert Systems

Heuristic finite-state agents (Berserker Aggro, Control Mage, Balanced Tactician) encoded with domain knowledge to execute high-synergy status rotations (e.g. Stun into Heavy into Rage).
